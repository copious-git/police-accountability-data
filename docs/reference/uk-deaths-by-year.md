# `uk/deaths-following-police-contact-by-year.csv`

**21 rows. One per financial year, 2004/05 to 2024/25.** Source: Independent Office for Police
Conduct time-series tables, Open Government Licence v3.0.

| Column | Type | Unit | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `year` | text  | n/a | UK financial year, `YYYY/YY` |
| `deaths` | integer | deaths | All categories combined, England and Wales |

## This series does not measure one constant thing across 21 years

The IOPC footnotes two year columns in its own tables, and both changes affect the
"deaths following other police contact" category:

- **2010/11**, a change in definition, narrowing the category.
- **2015/16**, an expansion of IOPC investigative resource and capacity.

**A rise after either year reflects, at least in part, what the IOPC counted and how much of it it
had the capacity to investigate.** Do not read 2004/05 against 2024/25 as like for like, and do not
describe a change across those boundaries as a trend in deaths.

## Corrected 2026-08-31

2010/11 and 2015/16 were previously published as 95 and 99. They are **152** and **205**. See
[Corrections](../corrections.md). Anyone holding a copy downloaded before that date should replace it.
